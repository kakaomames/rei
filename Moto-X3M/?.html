    Moto X3M

    What is the Moto X3M game?

    Moto X3M is a high-octane motorcycle racing game developed by MadPuffers that challenges players to navigate intense, obstacle-filled tracks with speed, skill, and precision. In this adrenaline-fueled time-trial experience, riders must race against the clock, dodging deadly traps, launching off massive ramps, and performing daring stunts to shave precious seconds off their time.

    Each of the game’s 22 levels presents unique challenges, from spinning saw blades to explosive obstacles, testing both reflexes and strategy. The riskier the stunts and the faster the completion time, the more stars players earn—these stars can then be used to unlock new bikes. With smooth physics, responsive controls, and the ability to accelerate, brake, and tilt mid-air, every jump and landing becomes a crucial part of the race.

    Moto X3M’s dynamic gameplay and time-based challenges keep players engaged as they push for better times and perfect their racing techniques. Whether carefully maneuvering through perilous obstacles or going full throttle to set record-breaking speeds, mastering balance and control is key. A favorite among motorcycle and stunt-racing enthusiasts, the game delivers an exhilarating and rewarding experience from start to finish.

    How to Play

    • Up (↑) to accelerate
    • Down (↓) to brake or reverse
    • Left (←) to tilt backward (used for backflips or adjusting balance in the air)
    • Right (→) to tilt forward (used for front flips or adjusting balance)
    • Performing flips and stunts in the air can reduce your overall time if done correctly
    • Properly adjusting your bike’s tilt helps land safely and maintain momentum
    • Crashing will reset the player at the last checkpoint
